The people of Medari were aware of dragons in the way one might be aware of elephants, say, or blue whales, or perhaps even dinosaurs. The average person was unlikely to have seen one in the wild, and if one ever *were* to be seen outside of a zoo or museum, it was generally a sign that you were about to have a very bad day. At least, that was the prevailing assumption. In truth, most dragons were actually quite docile, but, owing to their enormous size and incredible magical prowess, most people were unwilling to find this out firsthand, instead assuming that the mere presence of a being capable of leveling an entire city was a threat to do so. Consequently, dragons tended to be rather wary of humans.
