# Background
## Why is it called MONO?
The initial idea was to write an album that could pass as a video game soundtrack, but in order to do that, it would help to know the plot of the game. I wasn't going to make the actual game, so it didn't matter much if the plot was derivative. So, to keep things simple (haha), I decided the plot could just be the hero's journey. The hero's journey is also known as the monomyth, and I thought it would be funny to have an album titled "MONO (Stereo)". Hence, MONO.

Now that I actually am writing the story with the intention of publishing it, I'm realizing maybe it's not the best name. I'm no longer deliberately adhering to the hero's journey, and it doesn't have any in-universe meaning that I can recall, but I don't have any alternatives yet, either.

## Other Stuff to Know
Some of this will probably be apparent through reading the story, but it might be helpful to know it beforehand.

- It was initially envisioned as a video game, but now it's probably going to end up being a webcomic.
- It's not really a sci-fi story, but it does take place on a different planet called Medari. It's fairly similar to Earth, but it has multiple moons.
- Magic exists, and anyone can learn to use it. It can be dangerous, though. Casting a spell above your level can kill you, for example.
- All of the characters are furries. This doesn't really have much impact on the plot except when it's funny.
- All of the characters are queer. This does have an impact on the plot. Homophobia, transphobia, etc. aren't issues in this world.
	- There's a token straight guy, though. He dies in some embarrassing, slapstick-y fashion.

[[Luke Campbell]] is an ordinary high schooler. He lives in a small rural town called [[Genton]], which he finds utterly boring. One night, he has a dream about a [[Ednathrallor Verdensmorder|dragon]] attacking the [[Plata Fairgrounds|Renaissance Faire]], which he plans to attend the next day. He decides to head there as well. He's not too worried about the dragon—it was just a dream, after all—but he would like to know where Sam is, and besides, they left their phone behind.

[^sam]: Sam used to be Luke's love interest before we decided to make them siblings instead. Also, they are nonbinary and use they/them pronouns.

Oops, turns out it wasn't just a dream but a vision. A dragon shows up, and everyone panics. Notably, the dragon isn't actually attacking anyone; people are just panicking because they *assume* she's going to do something. Luke manages to stay calm, but it doesn't do much good when everyone else is completely losing their shit; he gets knocked down into the mud by a stampede of people and is about to be trampled when suddenly he begins to float away. Is he dying? No! He's being rescued!

A boy about his age has cast a spell around him that's essentially a giant bubble. The bubble carries him safely away from the crowd before it pops, unceremoniously dumping him back into the mud. Despite this, Luke is just happy to still be alive, and he thanks his mysterious savior profusely; using magic can be risky, so it's rather noble to use it to save a stranger.

The other boy plays it cool: it's no big deal, it's just the right thing to do, etc. Besides, he invented the spell and figured this was an ideal opportunity to test it out. This instantly promotes him to Coolest Guy Ever in Luke's view, much to his chagrin.

(Imagine Shrek and Donkey. That's basically the vibe here. Also like Shrek, the dragon and the horse are eventually going to fuck.)

Eventually, Luke realizes he's never actually introduced himself, so he rectifies this forthwith. His new idol's name is [[Case Weston]], and he's an apprentice mage who also works at the nearby [[The Old Silver Mine|museum]], which happens to be owned by his mentor, [[James Redawić]]. Sensing an opportunity to learn more about the dragon and Sam's potential whereabouts, Luke asks Case to introduce him to James. Sensing an opportunity to be rid of this overeager chatterbox, Case happily obliges.

Luke asks James if it's possible the dragon kidnapped Sam. James basically tells Luke that he's being racist towards dragons, and he's about to kick Luke out when Luke mentions foreseeing the dragon "attack" the night before. James's tone abruptly shifts, and he tells Luke to sit back down. He has an idea about where Sam might be: it wasn't a dragon that kidnapped them, but the evil wizard, [[CURSED AL]].

Luke is incredulous; James has essentially told him his sibling has been taken by the bogeyman. CURSED AL isn't real! That's just a story parents make up to get their children to behave. James insists this is the most likely case. After all, Luke probably didn't believe dragons were real before today either. Luke is again in disbelief. How could he be THE CHOSEN ONE? He's just an ordinary kid with no real talents to speak of; he hardly even knows magic! "Uh... hello? You literally had a dream about the future. What do you mean you're not special?"

Luke doesn't have a counter to this, but he's still not convinced. Unfortunately, "Refusal of the Call" is classic CHOSEN ONE behavior, so he really has no other option. (He's THE *CHOSEN* ONE, not THE ONE WHO CHOSE. He doesn't really get a say.)

However, there is a theoretical way to combine SOULs such that they can handle the necessary mana. In order to do this, AL will need the seven Legendary Artifacts (and a number of human sacrifices). Luke's job, therefore, is to get the Artifacts before AL does.[^james] He has until the moons align (about a month) to do this.[^moons]

[^james]: Why doesn't James just do this himself? For one, he's in a wheelchair, and magic can't heal him.
