Tolvanaite (lit. *People's tongue*), also known as High Draconic, is the language spoken by dragons and celestial beings.

# Grammar
Tolvanaite has no grammatical gender outside of personal pronouns. Owing to dragons' tendencies to hoard things, it's generally assumed that one is speaking in the plural, thus Tolvanaite does not distinguish between singular and plural. Similar to Russian, the language lacks articles.

## Case System
Similar to Latin or Russian, Tolvanaite has a case system. The following cases are used:

- Nominative - marks the subject of a verb; generally the form seen in dictionaries
- Accusative - marks the direct object
- Dative - marks the indirect object
- Genitive - marks possession, composition, origin, etc.
- Instrumental - indicates that a noun is the _instrument_ or means by or with which the subject achieves or accomplishes an action
- Prepositional - marks the object of a preposition
- Vocative - marks the person being addressed

Because of the case system, word order is not as rigid as it is in other languages such as English, where "John hit Jack" and "Jack hit John" have opposite meanings. Though words in Tolvanaite can theoretically be placed in any order, sentences most often follow the pattern of Subject-Object-Verb.
